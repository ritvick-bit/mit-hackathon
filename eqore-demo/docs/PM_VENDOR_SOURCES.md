Checked 2026-11-07. These public primary sources support workflow categories and authority boundaries. The application is not an EQORE internal SOP, a licensed engineering review, or a verified utility service-territory assignment.

## Three distinct demonstration situations

1. **Harbor Components — delayed delivery and replacement rigging.** Equipment ETA misses the booked unloading slot. Request qualified replacement rigging options for the required equipment/site scope and window; compare available dates, quote scope and price. Keep confirmed physical receipt, facility access and lift/site readiness separate from choosing or approving a vendor.
2. **Millbrook Cold Storage — EPC/interconnection package discrepancy.** The engineer must reconcile equipment information, the issued single-line and the utility application while preserving the customer's refrigeration constraints. Request scoped engineering assistance and show its document turnaround. Utility review duration remains unknown; buying faster engineering cannot promise utility approval or an earlier operating date.
3. **Riverbend Logistics — electrical field discrepancy before commissioning.** A field report identifies a CT/metering mismatch against the issued design. Request a qualified electrical corrective-work package, responsible engineer coordination and independent verification/retest. A photo or contractor completion statement alone does not establish correct protection, safe operation, or utility authorization.

## Explicit demo design assumptions

- Vendor ranking combines capability, geography, current qualifications, resource fit and dated commercial responses before comparing speed, price and past performance. The weighting is a product decision, not a published EQORE policy.
- Performance figures and reputation notes are invented internal profile data; do not describe them as live marketplace reviews.
- Inquiry messages and vendor replies are local simulation records. No real email, purchase order, booking or external contact occurs in this demo.
- A recommendation is not an approval. A PM approval is scoped to the displayed proposal and authority limit; a later commitment must recheck quote validity, project revision and the proposed window.
- An accepted plan is not a confirmed crew booking, physical work completion, inspection acceptance or authorization to operate. Those states need their own evidence.
- A quote expiry, changed availability, design revision, price change, missing scope or unverified qualification must invalidate the affected option rather than silently substituting a new one under an old approval.
- The short LLM summary is a reading aid. Preserve original text, attachments, sender, time and source links; raw observations and deterministic validation remain separate from that summary.

## Material failure cases to exercise

Exercise duplicate inquiry attempts, stale approvals, cross-project actions, expired quotes, changed resource availability, quotes outside the requested scope, vendor claims of utility permission, unauthorized PM over-limit approval, and approval without subsequent booking or completion. An unresolved original issue remains visible after rejection or a clarification request.
